# Opname360 has its own POS and replaces Smartlink

The shop's cashier app, Smartlink, is built for laundry businesses and has no public API, so its sales could only reach Opname360 through manual exports. Stock Opname Variances and the owner's hourly Profit and Loss both need every sale recorded the moment it happens, so Opname360 includes its own POS and Smartlink is retired. Integrating with another retail POS was not pursued, since stock, sales and the Ledger must share one record of each Stock Movement.
