# Stock Opname does not freeze sales

The shop stays open while staff count. Each Opname Line records the System Quantity at the moment that SKU is counted, and its Variance is measured against that snapshot; sales afterwards move stock as usual. Counting is blind and nothing changes the System Quantity until the session is approved, so Checkers never adjust stock themselves. Freezing the whole shop (the usual approach) was rejected because the owner wants frequent Partial Opnames of fast-moving SKUs during opening hours.
