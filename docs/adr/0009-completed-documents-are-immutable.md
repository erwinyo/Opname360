# Completed documents are never edited; mistakes are reversed

A completed Receipt, Goods Receipt, Production Receipt, Supplier Return, approved Stock Opname, Cash-out or closed Shift can't be edited or deleted, by anyone. A mistake is corrected by a Reversal document that points to the original, and every action is stamped with who did it and when in the Activity Log. Plain edit and delete would be simpler, but the Owner isn't on site, and the numbers are only trustworthy if nobody, Admin included, can quietly rewrite yesterday's stock or cash.
