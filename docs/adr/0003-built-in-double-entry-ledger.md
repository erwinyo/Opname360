# Bookkeeping is a double-entry Ledger inside Opname360

The owner wants a T-account Balance Sheet, which only double-entry books can produce. Rather than exporting journals to Accurate or Jurnal.id, Opname360 keeps its own Ledger and posts entries automatically from stock, sales, opname and cash events, so stock value and the books cannot drift apart. The Ledger is built after the stock core; stock events carry their cost from day one so their journal entries can be generated later.
